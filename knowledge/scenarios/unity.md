# Unity Cup: Shine On, Team Spirit!

**ID:** `scenario:unity` · **Bot alias:** `Unity Cup`  
**JP:** アオハル杯 (Aoharu Cup) · Global 2025-11-06  
**Caps:** 1300 / 1300 / 1300 / 1300 / **1800 Wit** · **Spark:** Unity Cup (Power + Wit)

## Loop

Teammates join over the run (no friendship gauges). **Spirit gauge** fills via Unity Training; full gauge → **Spirit Burst** (huge stats + hints + team stats, high Energy cost; Wit recovers Energy). After July 2026 overhaul: post-burst teammates can **Extreme Spirit Burst** (purple): more stats, higher teammate caps, 0% fail, Ignited Spirit hint.

## Structure

Five Unity Cup team races (5 legs) every six months; Finals vs Team Zenith (Riko Kashimoto). Winning 4th Preseason vs Elite Team upgrades Zenith + spark odds.

## Links

## Skills

Burning Spirit (10+ bursts) vs Ignited Spirit (≤9 + matching Extreme) × five stats; only Ignited inheritable.

## Training notes

Plan bursts around Energy; Extreme bursts are high priority when available. Wit facility is both recovery and a path to the raised Wit cap.
